Expiry · Home redesign: three options

Real-size mock-ups in Expiry's own colours. Each option shows the folded cover screen (375 px) and the unfolded inner screen (820 px). Scroll sideways inside a row to see the unfolded screen on a phone. All three include the design review's common fixes: a sync status pill instead of a Sync button, real dates on rows, a calendar warning that only clears when updated, and Renew as the main button on expired documents. Numbered dots point to what's new.

B · Timeline

Home answers “what do I need to do, and what's coming?”. A short To-do card at the top, then a 12-month timeline of every reminder and expiry, month by month. A switch at the top shows the full list by status when you want it.

Best for: planning ahead; replaces the mostly-empty month gridEffort: mediumTrade-off: documents more than 12 months away are only in “All documents”

A + C combined · A's list folded, C's family view unfolded

Folded, Home is design A's list: everything in one place, with the sync pill, slim counters and dates. Unfolded, the list stays on the left and the right pane, instead of “Select a document”, shows the family overview from C. Tap a document and its details replace the overview; close it (or tap the family icon) and the overview comes back.

Best for: the familiar list on the cover screen, and the wasted unfolded space put to workEffort: small to mediumTrade-off: the family view is only on the inner screen (folded, the person chips filter the list instead)
