How it behaves

  1. Settings opens with all sections closed. Sections you opened stay open until you close them or leave Expiry.
  2. Opening a section scrolls just enough to show its top; nothing else moves.
  3. The “Synced” pill and the backup/sync notices open Settings with Your data already open.
  4. Automatic sync redraws keep your open sections and scroll position.
  5. Sub-screens (a person, document types, restore) still open as their own page, as now.
